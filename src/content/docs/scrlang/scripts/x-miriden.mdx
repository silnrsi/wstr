## Script description

The Miriden script was invented in 2011 by Yacouba Diakité for the Maninka language of Guinea.

## Unicode status

The Miriden alphabet is not yet in Unicode. It is not yet in the Roadmap for the Unicode Standard. The [Script Encoding Initiative](https://sei.berkeley.edu/) includes this script in their list of “Scripts to Encode”. Contributions here may support their efforts.
