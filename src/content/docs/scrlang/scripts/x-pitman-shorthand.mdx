## Script description

Pitman Shorthand is a system of writing based on pronunciation of English words and is not based on the spelling of words. It was originally developed by the Englishman Sir Isaac Pitman, and it was published in 1837. The script is on the Script Encoding Initiative's list of scripts to encode.

## Unicode status

Pitman Shorthand is not yet in Unicode. It is not yet in the Roadmap for the Unicode Standard. The [Script Encoding Initiative](https://sei.berkeley.edu/) includes this script in their list of “Scripts to Encode”. Contributions here may support their efforts.
